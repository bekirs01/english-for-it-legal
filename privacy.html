Privacy Policy

This policy describes English for IT: Words, Phrases for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. It matches the app as shipped: an offline study tool with no account, no advertising, and no analytics.

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Bekir Suçkaran. Contact: bekirsucikaran00@gmail.com.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not upload your vocabulary, favorites, quiz answers, or study progress to a server operated for this app.

Data stored on your devices

Learning content is installed with the app. On iPhone, the app stores your translation language, sound and haptic preferences, favorites, learned items, wrong answers, and streak in local storage on that device. Deleting the app deletes that local data.

If you send a learning list to Apple Watch, the iPhone transfers up to 24 words or phrases to the Watch with Watch Connectivity. The Watch keeps that list in a local file on the Watch. The transfer does not go through a separate cloud account for this app.

Reminders

Reminders are optional. When you turn them on and send a list, the Watch schedules local notifications for the time window you chose on iPhone. The Watch asks for notification permission before those reminders can appear. The notification text is a short practice prompt in your selected language. It does not include a remote push service. Turning reminders off and sending again removes the pending reminders created by this app. iOS Focus, Low Power Mode, and authorization settings can still delay or hide a notification.

Speech and sounds

English pronunciation uses the speech voice already on your device. The app does not record your voice and does not send text to a speech server. Sound effects are audio files inside the app.

Privacy manifest

The iPhone and Watch apps declare no tracking and no collected data types. They access UserDefaults only to store this app’s own settings and the pending Watch plan (reason CA92.1). Required-reason declarations that belong to Apple’s and Flutter’s system frameworks are included with those frameworks.

Children

The app is a language-study tool. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new date.
