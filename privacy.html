Privacy Policy

This policy describes English for IT: Words, Phrases (the app shown on your device as English for IT) for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. It matches the application reviewed in source on 14 October 2026.

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

Application identity

English for IT is an offline language-study app for software developers, programmers, IT professionals, and students. The publisher named in this project’s legal pages is Bekir Suçkaran. Support contact: bekirsucikaran00@gmail.com.

Data collection

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not include advertising. It does not include an analytics or tracking SDK. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. It does not upload vocabulary, favorites, quiz answers, streak, or settings to a server operated for this app. The reviewed source does not call a network API for learning content.

Local learning information

The curriculum is installed with the app: 1,000 IT words, 500 professional phrases, 25 developer dialogues, and the interface text for 15 learner languages. That content is read from files inside the app. It is not downloaded at study time.

Favorites, progress, and settings

On iPhone and iPad, the app stores these items on the device with local preferences:

  • Translation language
  • Sound-effect and haptic preferences
  • Favorite words and phrases
  • Items you mark as learned
  • Quiz answers you missed, and later correct answers for those items
  • Daily streak
  • The Apple Watch learning list and reminder window you prepare on iPhone

Deleting the app deletes that local information.

Apple Watch synchronization

If you send a learning list to Apple Watch, the iPhone transfers up to 24 words or phrases through Watch Connectivity, together with the selected learner language and, when you enable them, the reminder window. The Watch stores that list in a local file on the Watch. The transfer does not go through a separate cloud account for this app. Cards already sent remain on the Watch when the iPhone is temporarily disconnected.

Reminder notifications

Reminders are optional. When you turn them on and send a list, the Watch schedules local notifications for the time window you chose on iPhone. The Watch asks for notification permission before those reminders can appear. The notification text is a short practice prompt. It is not a remote push message. Turning reminders off and sending again removes the pending reminders created by this app. Focus, Low Power Mode, authorization, and wrist state can still delay or hide a notification.

Text-to-speech

English pronunciation uses the speech voice already on your device. The app does not record your voice and does not send the sentence to a speech server. Sound effects are audio files shipped inside the app. You can turn sound effects off in Settings.

Third-party services

The app is built with Flutter and uses on-device plugins for local preferences, bundled sound effects, and system speech. Those plugins are not advertising or analytics services. No third-party account, payment, or tracking service is part of the reviewed build.

Data retention

Learning progress stays on your devices until you delete the app, clear the Watch learning list from the iPhone screen, or remove the Watch app. The developer does not keep a copy, because the app does not send that information to a developer server.

User controls

In Settings you can change the translation language and turn sound effects and haptics on or off. On the Apple Watch screen you can change the learning list, turn reminders on or off, and clear the list. You can also refuse the Watch notification prompt. Deleting the iPhone app or the Watch app removes the local copy on that device.

Children

The app is a language-study tool. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children, and it does not ask for an account.

Privacy manifest

The iPhone and Watch apps declare no tracking and no collected data types. Their own code reads and writes UserDefaults only for information the app itself stores, declared with reason CA92.1. The local-preferences plugin ships its own privacy manifest. Frameworks from Apple and Flutter include the declarations that belong to those frameworks.

Policy updates

If this policy changes, the updated text will replace this page and the date above will change.
