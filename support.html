Support

English for IT is an offline course for the English used by software teams. It includes 1,000 IT words, 500 professional phrases, 25 developer dialogues, quizzes, flashcards, on-device pronunciation, and an Apple Watch companion.

Email bekirsucikaran00@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, Apple Watch model if the question is about the Watch, and what you expected to happen.

Installation

Install English for IT from the App Store on an iPhone or iPad. The Apple Watch app is installed with the iPhone app when a paired Watch is present. Open the iPhone app once before you send a learning list to the Watch. The course does not require an account.

Vocabulary and phrases

From Home, open Vocabulary or Phrases. Each category has 50 items. Tap the speaker to hear the English line. Use the heart to save a favorite and the check to mark an item as learned. Favorites are on the Favorites tab.

Dialogues

Open Dialogues and choose a conversation. Each bubble is one line. Tap the speaker on a line to hear it. Return to the list to open another conversation. The app does not place a live call or grade your speaking.

Language selection

The language you are learning is English. In Settings, Translation language changes the meanings and the interface. The choices are English, Turkish, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Indonesian, and Persian. Arabic and Persian use a right-to-left layout. A quiz that is already open keeps the language it started with. The next quiz uses the new language.

Audio

Pronunciation uses the English voice installed on the device. If a voice sounds incomplete, download a higher-quality English voice in the system Settings, under Accessibility or Spoken Content, depending on your iOS version. The app does not download a voice for you. If the speaker does nothing, check the silent switch and the volume, then turn Sound effects off and on again in the app Settings. Leave the app and the speech stops, because the app does not play audio in the background.

Quizzes and flashcards

Open Quiz from Home, or the quiz button inside a category. Choose a format when more than one is available. Missed answers are kept on the device and listed under Wrong Answers. Flashcards open from a category. Tap the card to turn it from the English line to the meaning. Mark a card learned, save it, or move to the next card.

Apple Watch setup

On iPhone, open Apple Watch. Choose up to 24 words or phrases, then send them. The Watch shows the English line and the meaning for the language selected on iPhone. Cards that were already sent remain on the Watch if the iPhone is temporarily disconnected.

Watch synchronization

Keep the iPhone and Watch paired and nearby, with Bluetooth on, the first time you send a list. If the Watch still shows the setup message, open the Watch app, then send the list again from the iPhone. Sending again replaces the list on the Watch. Clear the list on the iPhone screen to remove it.

Reminder notifications

Reminders are optional. Turn them on, choose the time window and how often they repeat, then send the list. The Watch asks for notification permission when a reminder schedule is first enabled. Allow notifications for English for IT on the Watch if you want them to appear. Focus, Low Power Mode, and wrist detection can still delay a notification. Turn reminders off and send again to remove the pending reminders created by this app.

Offline usage

Words, phrases, dialogues, quizzes, and flashcards work without a network connection. A Watch list that was already sent also stays on the Watch without a network connection. You do not sign in, and progress is not copied to a developer server. Deleting the app deletes the local progress on that device.
