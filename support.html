Support

Email bekirsucikaran00@gmail.com for help with English for IT: Words, Phrases. Please include your iOS version, your Apple Watch model if the question is about the Watch, and a short description of what you expected to happen.

Study without an account

You do not sign in. Words, phrases, and dialogues are installed with the app. Favorites, progress, the streak, and language settings stay on the device. Deleting the app deletes that local progress.

Languages

The language you are learning is English. Meanings and interface text can follow the translation language you choose in Settings: English, Turkish, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi, Indonesian, or Persian. Arabic and Persian use a right-to-left layout.

Pronunciation

Spoken audio uses the English voice on your device. Download a high-quality English voice in iOS Settings if the system voice sounds incomplete. The app does not download a voice for you.

Apple Watch

On iPhone, open Apple Watch, choose up to 24 words or phrases, and send them. The Watch shows the English term and the meaning for the language selected on iPhone. Cards that were already sent remain on the Watch if the iPhone is temporarily disconnected.

Reminders are optional. Turn them on, choose the time window and daily count, and send the list again. The Watch asks for notification permission when a reminder schedule is first enabled. Allow notifications on the Watch if you want the system to present them. Focus, Low Power Mode, and wrist state can still delay a notification. Turn reminders off and send again to remove the pending reminders created by this app.
