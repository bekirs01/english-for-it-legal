English for IT

An offline learning app for software developers, IT professionals, and computer science students who want to practice the English used at work.

The app includes 1,000 IT words, 500 professional phrases, 25 developer dialogues, quizzes, flashcards, on-device English pronunciation, and an Apple Watch companion. Learning content, favorites, and progress stay on the device.
